# Speedrun Test - Neon Night Racer

Create a polished, fast and fun **retro arcade racing game** called **Neon Night Racer** that runs in the browser.

The player drives a sports car at night down an endless highway through a neon synthwave city in the style of an 80s arcade cabinet: glowing grid, purple and pink sunset, palm trees, city skyline, light trails, rain-wet reflections.

The core fantasy is:

**the feeling of insane speed at night, one mistake away from a crash.**

Requirements:

- Responsive keyboard controls with a strong sense of speed (camera, motion, road lines, sound, screen effects).
- Traffic and obstacles that make the player react, dodge and take risks. Difficulty grows over time.
- At least one risk/reward mechanic (for example: near-miss bonus, boost, combo multiplier). Choose it yourself.
- Score, best score, a crash moment with impact, a game over screen and instant restart.
- Music and sound effects generated in code (no external audio files).
- The game must look like a finished arcade game, not a prototype with placeholder shapes.
- Deliver one `index.html` that runs from a simple local HTTP server. Libraries from a public CDN are allowed. No external images, models or audio files.

Gameplay details, camera, visual style, balancing and all technical decisions are yours.

Do not ask questions. Build it, run it, play it yourself, and fix anything that breaks before finishing.

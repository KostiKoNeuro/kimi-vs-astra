# Visual Test - Night Market Voxel Diorama

Create a beautiful, atmospheric **3D voxel diorama of a busy night market in Shenzhen**, rendered in the browser.

Picture a small, dense block of the city at night after the rain: food stalls with steam rising from woks and noodle pots, glowing neon signs and paper lanterns, tangled overhead wires, scooters weaving through, small voxel people walking, eating and bargaining, wet ground reflecting the lights, residential towers with lit windows rising behind.

The core fantasy is:

**a tiny living world you want to pause and look at for a long time.**

Requirements:

- Everything is built from voxels (cubes) and generated procedurally in code. No external models, images or textures.
- The scene must feel alive: moving people and scooters, flickering signs, steam, smoke or light rain, small details that reward a closer look.
- **Cinematic mode by default:** when the page opens, the camera performs a smooth, seamless **30-second looping flythrough** that shows the best views of the diorama. No UI visible in this mode.
- Press **C** to switch to free orbit camera control, press **C** again to return to the cinematic loop.
- Lighting and mood are the priority: night atmosphere, warm and neon light sources, glow, depth.
- Smooth performance on a modern desktop.
- Deliver one `index.html` that runs from a simple local HTTP server. Libraries from a public CDN are allowed.

Composition, details, color palette, camera path and all technical decisions are yours.

Do not ask questions. Build it, run it, watch the full 30-second loop, and fix anything that looks broken before finishing.
